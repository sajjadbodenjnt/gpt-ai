# Intended use and safety

AIBondhu is experimental research/health-education software and is not validated for clinical use.

Never rely on generated text to diagnose, triage, prescribe, calculate doses, interpret an individual's result, or delay emergency care. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional.

The model can hallucinate, omit caveats, reproduce errors, encode bias, or generate unsafe advice. Low validation loss/perplexity does not establish medical correctness. Any future clinical application requires independent clinical validation, privacy/security review, bias and robustness testing, human oversight, and applicable regulatory approval.
